Welcome to the landmarks of Tivat

Small town in Boka-Kotorska with an interesting landmarks. Lets find out something about town and it's landmarks!

Unlock exclusive content by today!

  • Short description of town

    • Tivat is a charming coastal town located in the Bay of Kotor, Montenegro. Known for its stunning natural beauty, Tivat offers a mix of modern amenities and traditional Mediterranean charm. It is home to the luxurious Porto Montenegro marina, attracting yachting enthusiasts and tourists with upscale restaurants, boutiques, and waterfront views. Tivat also boasts beautiful beaches, lush parks, and a rich cultural history, including landmarks like the Renaissance Summer House Buca and the 19th-century Church of St. Sava. The town serves as a peaceful gateway to explore the scenic bay and the nearby mountains.

  • Island of Flowers

    • Island of Flowers - MONASTERY OF SAINT ARCHANGEL MICHAEL is an Orthodox monastery located on the peninsula of Tivatska Prevlaka (Island of Flowers), opposite Tivat. This monastery was built on the remains of the Benedictine temple. From the 9th to the 14th century, it significantly marked the epoch of development of the city of Tivat. In the 12th century, Saint Sava enthroned the bishopric of Zeta in this very place. In 1452, when the Venetians came to Boka, they demolished the monastery, but its remains are still there today. Mosaics from the ancient period were found in the same place. In the 19th century, Countess Ekatarina Vlastelinović built the Church of the Holy Trinity on the site where the monastery used to be. On the island of Cijeća there is a magnificent beach about 1200 m long that stretches around the entire island. The beach itself consists of sand and gravel. The beach is surrounded by lush vegetation. Flower Island (Prevlaka) is located in the Tivat Bay near Tivat Airport. The island is about 1.5 kilometers from the popular tourist resort of Tivat and can be reached by boat from the city port or by bus, car or taxi. The size of the island is small - about 300 m long and 200 m wide. It is officially called an island, but it is more accurate to call it a peninsula, since it is connected to the mainland by a small isthmus.

  • Island of Our Lady of Mercy

    • The Island of Our Lady of Mercy is a small island located in the Tivat Bay. The length of this islet is 160 meters, and it is about 800 meters from the shore. On one side of the island, the depth of the sea is less than 1 meter, so it is possible to stand there. On the island there is the Catholic Church of Our Lady of Mercy, dedicated to the conception of the Holy Mother of God. There is also a small monastery, a bell tower and a courtyard surrounded by ramparts. The church and monastery were built in the 15th century, and priests who wanted to live in seclusion settled there. In 1524, Franciscans came to live in the monastery, where they stayed until the end of the 17th century. After that, the diocese of Kotor appoints two priests who will work and live on the island. A wooden statue of the miracle-working Mother of God, Our Lady of Mercy, is kept in the church. Our Lady of Mercy Island is located near several interesting attractions. Excursions often include not only the island of St. Mark, but also the peninsula of Luštica, where visitors will be able to smell the incredible scents of Mediterranean herbs, wild pomegranates, olives, pines and oaks, grapes and figs.

  • Church of Our Lady of Angels

    • The Church of Our Lady of Anđela is located on the Verige strait, across from Perast. The church belongs to the municipality of Tivat. Among the locals, this church is called Gospa od Oriza, i.e. "of the smile". The sailors, when they would return from long journeys and sail into "their own waters", would smile so contentedly, and the first thing they saw when sailing in was this small church on the rock. The exact year of construction is not known, but it is assumed to be 1585. Next to the church is a small defensive wall and loophole, and it is assumed that the church served as a watchtower and control of passage through the Verige Strait. Verige in the Bokek dialect means "passage" and it is precisely there that the cruise ships that sail to the port of Kotor pass. There is a small beach bar near the church, with pontoons and a pebble beach. From this place you can easily and quickly cross from one side of the coast to the other, because the width of the "Verige" passage is only 250 meters.

  • Interesting facts

    • From this place, if necessary, "chains" were raised and stretched from the sea, i.e. iron chains, with which enemy ships were blocked from entering the inner part of the Boka Kotorska Bay. This is where the current name for this strait comes from - Verige.
