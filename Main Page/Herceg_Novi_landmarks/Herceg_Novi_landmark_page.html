Welcome to the landmarks of Herceg Novi

Want to know in-depth about town of Herceg Novi? Why not start with some of the towns most famous landmarks!

Unlock exclusive content by today!

  • Kanli tower

    • Kanli tower fortress is located above the old town, along the main highway. There is no way around this place. Translated from the Turkish language, KANLI TOWER means BLOOD TOWER. The name of this tower was given by the Turks, when it was built, during the Turkish rule. In that period, the Kanli tower served as a cruel prison, where people who fought for the freedom of Montenegro were killed. The walls of this tower had seen a lot of human blood, it was easy to get in but to get out was almost impossible. The tower is located 85 meters above sea level, and the thickness of its walls is in some places as much as 20 meters. At the very entrance to the tower there are tunnels, rooms that served as prison cells, torture chambers for prisoners, water wells, as well as underground passages that connect all three towers in Herceg Novi, but which are not open to visitors. The staircase leads to the top of the tower, from where there is a magnificent view of the entire city. Today, this tower serves as an open-air amphitheater for numerous concerts, plays, and its capacity is 15,000 people, with a total of 1,000 seats.

  • Clock tower

    • The clock tower is located in the old town, i.e. the center of Herceg Novi. The clock tower is one of the symbols of the city. It was built in 1667, by order of Sultan Mahmud, and it has been showing the correct time for more than 300 years. During the Ottoman rule, there was a mosque next to the tower. Built during the reign of the Turks, the clock tower showed when it was time for Muslim prayer, but it also represented the main city gate, through which one passed from one part of the city to another. Today, the clock tower divides the main pedestrian street in Herceg Novi, Njegoševo, from Belavista square. Today, the tower is the trademark of Herceg Novi, whose image is also in the city's coat of arms. On the clock tower there is a bell from 1753, which was a gift from the Russian empress Catherine II. In the passage under the tower, one can see an unusual sculpture of the Mother of God, called the "Black Virgin", which is made of burnt wood. This monument is dedicated to the founder of the city. In front of or below the clock tower there is one of two large squares, the main square of Nikola Đurković, where there are numerous bars, restaurants, banks, shops and a staircase that leads to Škvera - the city port.

  • Španjola

    • In 1538, the Spanish fleet of Admiral Andrea Dori sailed into Boka Kotorska, more precisely into the port of Herceg Novi. The Turks immediately handed over the city to the Spaniards, who built a fortress in just one year. Already in 1539, the cruel pirate Hajrudin Barbarossa conquered Herceg Novi. After that, the Turkish Sultan Suleiman wanted to change the look of the fortress and to strengthen it further. The fortress today has 4 large bastions on the corners. The Turks, who did not build, but stole the fortress, were very proud of it. Above the entrance to the fortress, there used to be an inscription in the Turkish language, which read: "And the poets, who will be brought here by the road, looking at this fortress, will enthusiastically say: "Yes, what a great, beautiful building this is!" Of course, during its history, the fortress changed several owners, from the Republic of Venice, through Napoleon's army, the Austro-Hungarians and the Montenegrins. It is interesting that during history, this fortress served as a small town. (It used to be called "Upper Town") There was a water source, a bakery, systems for life. Today, the fortress is abandoned, but it is very interesting to see the remains of its history, which still exist here. In addition, the fortress offers a view of both remaining fortresses and the entire city of Herceg Novi. In the summer months, rave music festivals are held at the fortress, in a slightly different environment, compared to the usual discos and clubs.

  • Mare Fortress

    • Forte Mare Fortress, translated means "Sea Fortress". It is located next to the city port of Škver, on the sea coast. This fortress can be reached from the main town square, down a staircase that leads to the sea. The tower was built in the 14th century in order to defend the city in case of an attack from the sea. It is assumed that the foundation stone was laid by King Tvrtko I Kotromanić. Once upon a time, this fortress had a special berth for ships. During the Turkish and Venetian rule, the fortress was called KULA ABASPAŠA, which would mean Mighty Fortress. Which is indeed the case even today, although it no longer serves the defense of the city, the strength of this defensive tower can be felt and seen in its walls. In the 19th century, the fortress was rebuilt by the Austrians, so its present appearance dates from that century. With secret passages and a staircase until the middle of the 20th century, the fortress served civilian purposes. In 1952, the fortress was turned into an open-air theater, where plays are held during the summer months, as well as thematic concerts of foreign music.
