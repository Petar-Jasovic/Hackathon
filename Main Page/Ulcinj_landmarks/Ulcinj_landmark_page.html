Welcome to the landmarks of Ulcinj

Want to know in-depth about town of Ulcinj? Why not start with some of the towns most famous landmarks!

  • Balšić Tower

    • The Blašić Tower is located in the old town. The Balšićs were a Montenegrin medieval dynasty that ruled the then Zeta (Montenegro near the Skadar region) from 1360 to 1421. After the collapse of the Serbian Empire, the Balšićs ruled Montenegro independently. Over time, they extended their rule to Metohija and Travunia. They were not addressed by titles (prince, king, duke), but only bore the title "sir". They were related to the Nemanjić dynasty. Ulcinj was chosen as the capital from which the Balšićs ruled, and the tower was built at the end of the 14th, beginning of the 15th century. Originally, the tower was built as a two-story building, and later the Turks added another, third floor. The tower is located at the highest point in the old town, offering a wonderful view of the sea. There is a gallery inside the tower, and it is used for reading poetry, ie organizing literary evenings. "Palace of Venice" is located near the Balšić tower, an imposing building. It is believed that this palace (which is now a luxury accommodation facility) was the palace where the Doge of Venice resided during the Venetian Republic. The Balšić family used this palace as their court. Nearby there is a stone house from the 14th century, named "Dvori Balšića".

  • Interesting facts

    • Before entering the old town of Ulcinj, there is TURBE. Turbe represented a "seal" from the rule of the Ottoman Empire. These turbes bear witness to the struggle of two brothers for the love of one woman. Unable to make up her mind, the woman jumped into the sea from the Ulcinj fortress, thus ending her life, but the duel between the two brothers continued. One died in the war, the other died. Turbe is located in the place where they fought and represents a sanctuary, which, according to popular belief, heals. Turbe is surrounded by bars, and many tourists and residents of Ulcinj, when they pass by, insert a coin for good luck.
