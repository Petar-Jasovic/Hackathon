Tours

Want to explore in a group or to get know town better? Well in that case we can request you quite few tours in each town

Tour list

  1. Ulcinj

    • Old Town Walking Tour

      • Explore Ulcinj’s ancient fortress, narrow streets, and rich history, including the city’s Ottoman and Venetian influences.

    • Ada Bojana River Tour

      • A boat trip along the Bojana River, exploring the delta region, with stops for swimming, birdwatching, and a visit to Ada Bojana Island.

    • Velika Plaža (Great Beach) & Kiteboarding Tour

      • Visit one of the longest sandy beaches in the Balkans, with opportunities for water sports, especially kiteboarding lessons.

  2. Bar

    • Bar Old Town & Stari Bar Tour

      • Explore the ruins of the ancient city of Stari Bar and its well-preserved medieval buildings, mosques, and churches.

    • Skadar Lake Boat Tour

      • A scenic boat trip on Lake Skadar, known for its stunning natural beauty and diverse wildlife. You can visit island monasteries and explore local wine villages.

    • Bar Wine Tour

      • Visit local wineries in the Bar region, with tastings of Montenegrin wines, especially the famous Vranac variety.

  3. Budva

    • Budva Old Town Walking Tour

      • Discover Budva’s charming old town with its Venetian walls, cobbled streets, and the historic Citadel.

    • Sveti Stefan Island Tour

      • Visit the luxurious island of Sveti Stefan (photo stop only as it's a private resort), and nearby beaches like Miločer.

    • Boka Bay Cruise

      • Take a boat from Budva to explore the beautiful Boka Bay, visiting places like the Blue Cave, Mamula Island, and Lady of the Rocks.

  4. Tivat

    • Porto Montenegro Marina Tour

      • Discover Budva’s charming old town with its Venetian walls, cobbled streets, and the historic Citadel.

    • Blue Cave & Submarine Base Tour

      • A boat tour to the Blue Cave near Lustica Peninsula, with stops at old submarine bunkers used during the Yugoslav era.

    • Lady of the Rocks & Perast Tour

      • A scenic boat trip from Tivat to visit the iconic man-made island of Lady of the Rocks, combined with a visit to the historic town of Perast.

  5. Kotor

    • Kotor Old Town & City Walls Tour

      • A walking tour of Kotor’s UNESCO-listed old town, with visits to the Cathedral of St. Tryphon, Maritime Museum, and climbing the city walls for panoramic views.

    • Kotor Bay Boat Tour

      • A boat tour of the bay with stops at Our Lady of the Rocks, Perast, and other coastal towns.

    • Kotor Kayak or SUP Tour

      • Paddle around Kotor Bay on a kayak or stand-up paddleboard, enjoying views of the medieval towns and surrounding mountains.

  6. Herceg Novi

    • Herceg Novi Old Town & Fortress Tour

      • Discover the coastal charm of Herceg Novi, including the Forte Mare and Kanli Kula fortresses, with beautiful views of the Adriatic.

    • Montenegro Blue Cave Tour

      • A boat trip from Herceg Novi to the Blue Cave, with opportunities for swimming in the crystal-clear waters.

    • Luštica Peninsula Olive Oil & Village Tour

      • Explore the traditional villages of the Luštica Peninsula, with visits to local olive oil farms for tastings and tours.
