  • Risan mosaics

    • The Roman mosaics in Risno are the remains of a villa from the 2nd century AD. Typologically, the villa urbana belonged to the type of city villas and is the most representative example of Roman architectural creativity on this coast of the Adriatic. The villa was found in 1930 by Dušan Vuksan, then director of the State Museum in Cetinje. Considering that the ancient Roman city was located on the right bank of the river Spila, and according to the configuration of the terrain that changed significantly over the centuries, due to earthquakes alone, he came to the conclusion that it reached all the way to the cape called Rtac. Accordingly, there could have been a certain number of monumental buildings on that location, as evidenced by the archaeological remains of a city that was completely destroyed by a strong earthquake at the end of the 6th or the beginning of the 7th century AD, during which most of it sank into the sea. The building was rectangular in shape. with, on the ground floor, 14 rooms made according to the system of entering from one to the other, with an atrium in the middle and a back yard in the eastern part. It is believed that it had a floor in the back part, that it had richly decorated walls in the al fresco technique, on which, according to the fashion of the time, motifs from mythology and the genre of the scene were depicted. The floors were decorated with mosaics, of which 7 have been preserved, and of which the decoration of the cubicles (private rooms) is the best preserved. On the floor of the room, which is believed to have been a bedroom, is a representation of Hypnos, the god of sleep. In the medallion in the middle, bordered by black tessellations, Hypnos is represented as a half-naked young man in a half-lying position with crossed legs and folded wings behind both shoulders. The cult of this deity originated from Greek mythology, and it was believed to be a beautiful winged youth who touches the eyes of tired people with a twig dipped in the river of oblivion and puts them to sleep. In addition to the mosaics, the remains of the lararium, the sanctuary of the lars - guardian spirits who were depicted as two dancing young men holding cornucopias in their hands, are also visible. Part of the ancient heritage of Rizon, the remains of the monumental buildings of the forum, is exhibited in the former back yard of the villa.

  • Banja Monastery

    • Banja Monastery is located at the very end of Risno, in the shade of a cypress tree, on the way to Perast. The monastery belongs to the Metropolitanate of Montenegrin and Littoral, of the Serbian Orthodox Church. The monastery was built in the 12th century by the ruler Stefan Nemanja. In the Monastery there is a small church dedicated to St. George, whose holy relics are still there today, silvered and gilded with an inscription. In 1654, the Turks demolished the original Nemanja Church, the remains of which are still visible today. During the seventeenth century, a new small church was built, also dedicated to St. George. The nobles of Risnjan donated a lot and invested in this place, in their monastery and church. Various benefactors donated gold, icons, numerous valuables. The biggest benefactor from Risno, Vaso Ćuković, donated a large bell to the monastery, worth 30,000 thousand dinars at the time, and later 340,000 dinars, for the reconstruction of the monastery. Numerous governments, especially Turkey, significantly impoverished the monastery, which was not destroyed, but was repeatedly ravaged. Today, the monastery houses one of the oldest icons in Boka, the work of the Rafailovic-Dimitrijevic iconographic house from the 17th century.

  • Gabela Street

    • Gabela Street is the oldest street in Risno, dating back to the 12th century, while some parts of it were built later during the Turkish occupation. The street stretches as much as 3 kilometers, "along the hill", from the coast, i.e. from the main square in Risno, where there is also a market, called Risanski Pazar. Along the street, which is very well preserved, there are old stone houses, which are a typical building style for the old town and Boka Kotorska.
