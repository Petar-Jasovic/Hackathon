Welcome to the landmarks of Kotor

Want to know in-depth about town of Kotor? Why not start with some of the towns most famous landmarks!

Unlock exclusive content by today!

  • Arms Square

    • Trg od Oružja is the main and largest square in Kotor. Trg od Oružja is the place for meetings, farewells and all major events in the city. Through the main city gate from the sea, you can reach the main city square. Every major event and celebration takes place here. The square got its name from Oružje, because weapons were repaired and stored in this place, during the Venetian Republic, in the Arsenal building, which is located at the very end of the square, in the northeastern part. The soldiers who defended and guarded the city would gather in this square and equip themselves with weapons for the defense of the city. The most famous defense of the city lasted 3 days, when in 1539, the Turkish pirate Hajrudin Barbarossa attacked Kotor. On the main square there is the Providur's Palace, the city guard tower, the old clock tower, the column of shame and the Napoleonic theater. Arms Square is always full of tourists and locals. Here are the main banks, cafes and restaurants, hotels (Hotel Cattaro and Hotel Vardar - the place where the oldest gentlemen of Kotor drink their morning coffee), souvenir shops and photo shops. In this city, everything starts from the main square, including the city tour itself. Numerous films have been filmed on Oružje Square, among which the most famous is "Dark Side of the Sun", in which American actor Brad Pitt plays the main role. The film was shot in 1987. It is interesting that the city of Kotor looks identical, back in 1987 and now.

  • Town tower and clock

    • The city clock tower is located on the main city Square of Arms. The town tower is one of the symbols of Kotor, and every small town on the coast, similar to Kotor, is decorated with a town tower with a clock. The tower was built in 1602, by order of the Venetian governor named Antonio Grimaldi. The clock tower is built of massive stone blocks, and on the third floor there are two large clocks on two sides, which always show the correct time. The bell of the clock can be heard every 30 minutes. If it is 10 o'clock in the morning, the clock will strike 10 times, if it is 10:30 a.m., the clock will strike only once. After the 1979 earthquake, the tower became slightly crooked. On the ground floor of the tower, there is an old watch shop, which is owned by the old Homen family, who moved to Kotor in the 19th century and whose generations still sell watches, but also take care of the mechanism of the old Kotor clock (the clock mechanism can be seen from the shop of the Homen family), so that the old clock would never stop ticking.

  • Cathedral of St. Tripun

    • Saint Tripun Cathedral is one of the symbols of Kotor. Saint Tripun is the patron saint of the city. The Cathedral of St. Tripun was built in 1166, on the site of a church also dedicated to St. Tripuna, but from IX. century. In one of the first earthquakes that hit Kotor, the old church was destroyed, and in its place, the people of Kotor built a cathedral and dedicated it to their patron saint. The people of Kotor like to say that the cathedral is much older, from 1166, which is true, because today inside it there are the remains of the old church. Inside the cathedral are the relics of Saint Tripun, brought from Constantinople to Kotor, in a silver coffin, which the work of master goldsmiths from Kotor. The founder of the cathedral is priest Andrea Saracenih, whose white marble sarcophagus is located in the cathedral. The cathedral is open to both tourists and guests. Every Sunday from 09:00 to 11:00 there is a holy mass in the cathedral. There are 3 large altars inside, and at the very end, something that catches the eye of the guests - it is a golden pala, the work of Kotor masters, which represents a real masterpiece from the 15th century. The life of the saint is represented in 12 pictures, on the pali. In front of the altar, there is a pink and white ciborium from the 14th century, under which the newlyweds vow their eternal love. It is assumed that the ciborium is the work of Fra Vito Kotoranin, who is the builder of Visoki Dečani. Sometimes it is possible to see Biedermeier left in the cathedral, because every bride who gets married in this place leaves a Biedermeier in the cathedral, believing that this act will bring her a happy marriage. On the second floor, after 30 steps, there is a small room that keeps the relics of St. Trypun and then the treasury of the Cathedral. The relics of the saint are brought out only once a year, in a procession through the old town, on the 4th of February. In the Cathedral treasury, it is possible to see the remains of the old church from the 9th century, as well as numerous relics made in Kotor from the 14th to the 17th century - the work of the Kotor goldsmiths. From the balcony of the cathedral there is a magnificent view of the square and the roofs of Kotor's houses.
